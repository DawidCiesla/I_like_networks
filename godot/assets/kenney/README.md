# Kenney City Kit subset

These curated Godot GLB imports come from Kenney's City Kit packs. Each kit is released under CC0 1.0; the original `License.txt` is kept beside its models. Crediting Kenney is optional.

Imported source packs:

- `suburban/`: City Kit Suburban 2.0, house and townhouse variants plus small and large trees.
- `commercial/`: City Kit Commercial 2.1, shops, midrise buildings and towers.
- `industrial/`: City Kit Industrial 2.0, workshops and warehouses.
- `roads/`: City Kit Roads 2.1, curved roadside light.

Only selected GLB models and the matching `models/Textures/colormap.png` atlases are included. FBX, OBJ, source previews and unused model variants remain in the supplied archives. The game keeps its semantic procedural road ribbons and uses the road-kit light as an instanced roadside detail.
